What triggers each alert (frozen momentum-scan-1.0):
F1 — N2 relative-strength continuation: on a closed 15m bar, coin's 8-bar return beats BTC's by >3%, BTC not falling (>−1%), and price not yet at a 20-bar high (pre-breakout).
F2 — top-gainer acceleration + pullback + confirm: coin is a top-3 trailing-8-bar gainer, short-run momentum is accelerating, the prior bar was a controlled pullback (closed in the lower half of its range), and the signal bar renews upward (green & above prior close).

How paper orders & stops work: entry fills at the OPEN of the bar AFTER signal detection (next executable price), with 0.08% fee + 0.05% slippage + half the recorded spread each side. Stops: A trails 2% below peak once profitable; B tightens 2%→1.5%→1% at +2/+4/+8% peak (up-only); C additionally sells 40% at +4% then trails the rest; D uses 1.5×ATR% at entry, clamped 1.5–6%. Within each new bar the stop is checked (adverse) BEFORE the bar's high raises it — no look-ahead; a bar that gaps below the stop fills at the (worse) open. Capital books cap at 3 slots and log missed entries when full; the matched layer takes every signal to isolate exit quality.
